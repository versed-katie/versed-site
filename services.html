The first 90 days, then ongoing.

  1. Month 1

    Map

After 90 days

What you'll have in hand.

  • Clean, reconciled books you can trust
  • A map of how work and money move through your business, and where they get stuck
  • A prioritized fix list, with the first fixes already in place
  • A dashboard with the numbers that matter to you
  • Documented processes your team can follow
  • A monthly rhythm, so your numbers show up on time and get talked through

This is for you if

  • you own a business doing roughly $400K to $3M a year, with a small team
  • there's no full-time finance or operations person, so it all runs through you
  • you want your numbers to help you make decisions, not just file your taxes
  • you answer questions quickly and want a partner, not a vendor

It's probably not the right fit if

  • you only need transactions categorized once a year at tax time
  • you already have an in-house controller or operations director
  • you'd rather hand it off and not hear about it again

Questions

Common questions

What happens after the first 90 days?

We move into the ongoing partnership: monthly books, reporting, a numbers conversation and one operations improvement each quarter. There's no long-term contract, and you can adjust the scope as your business changes.

Can I start with just the audit?

Yes. The audit works on its own, and if you decide to move into the full partnership afterward, it counts as your first month.

Do I need to use QuickBooks?

QuickBooks Online is what I recommend, and I can help you get set up. If you're on another platform, let's talk about it on your discovery call.

Do you only work with therapy practices?

No. I work with owner-led businesses of many kinds, with a focus on women-founded, women-run and minority-owned businesses. Therapy and wellness practices are still a big part of my client list.

How do you handle confidential information?

I work with your business financials: revenue, expenses and accounts. For practices that handle client health information, I typically don't see client names or clinical data, and I work with you to keep it that way.
