The problem

Somewhere along the way you also became the bookkeeper, the office manager and the person who knows where every spreadsheet lives.

Because of this, decisions get made on gut feel, the same manual tasks eat your week, and nobody is looking at the whole picture with you.

  • Books you don't trust

    They're behind, or they're current but something feels off.

  • Processes that live in your head

How it works

A steady monthly rhythm.

  3. Monthly close
